Add to Chrome

The extension pairs with the app on its own. Also works in Brave, Arc and Edge.

  1. In the app, open Settings → Connections → Chrome extension and click Copy path.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode in the top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked, press ⇧⌘G in the dialog, paste the copied path and confirm.
  4. That’s it. The extension pairs with the app on its own.
Watch your agents from your phone Wi-Fi / Tailscale
  1. Keep Agenteeq running on your Mac. You can close the window.
  2. In Settings → Open on phone, turn on access for your home network or for Tailscale.
  3. Click Create code for phone and scan the QR code with your camera. Your phone opens already paired.

Before you start.

Good answers before your first agent starts working.

How much does it cost?

During the beta, Agenteeq is free with every feature. We will publish pricing for the paid version before the beta ends.

Does it show what I spend on AI?

Yes. It recognises your Claude and ChatGPT plan on its own, with the tier and its list price. It loads API costs from the Anthropic and OpenAI Admin APIs, broken down by model. It never converts tokens into money, it shows only verified amounts.

Do I have to set anything up?

No. Agenteeq finds the tools on your Mac as soon as you start them. Add the extension for web chats, API costs or an account whenever you want them.

Can anyone else see my conversations?

No. Agenteeq reads transcripts only on your Mac and sends them nowhere. Account sync carries summary numbers only.

What about Windows?

The Windows version is in beta. Download it above; the install page lists how it differs from the Mac.
