Privacy by design

Your work
should stay yours.

Agenteeq has no cloud account for your transcripts, because it doesn’t need one. Everything is processed on your Mac, and the extension sends data only to the local app.

No account, no tracking

No sign-up, no analytics. Fonts and the interface ship with the app, so nothing is fetched from third-party servers. Optional APIs and AI services are yours to connect.

On your phone when you’re away

Turn on access on the same Wi-Fi or through your own Tailscale network and point your phone’s camera at the QR code in the app. No typing addresses or numbers.

Data only from a running Mac

Your Mac must be on, awake and running Agenteeq. A closed window is fine; a quit app or a Mac that is off can’t provide data.

Download for Mac

Apple Silicon · macOS 14 or later · latest release · 38 MB

  1. 1Unzip the download

    Agenteeq appears in your Downloads. Double-click to unzip it.

  2. 2Drag it to Applications

    That’s the whole installation. Nothing else is written anywhere.

  3. 3Allow it once in Settings

    macOS can’t verify the beta on its own yet. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Agenteeq and click Open Anyway.

Why the extra step? An Apple developer signature costs money every year and the beta doesn’t have one yet. Without it, macOS won’t open the app on its own and says it can’t verify it — that’s not a malware finding, just a missing stamp. You confirm it once in Settings; after that, Agenteeq opens with a double-click like any other app. Once we add the signature, this step goes away.

On macOS 14 the older shortcut also works: right-click and choose Open. Apple removed it in macOS 15 and later, which is why the steps above go through Settings — that works everywhere.

Add web chats with the Chrome extension 10 minutes
  1. In the app, open Settings → Connections → Chrome extension and click Copy path.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode in the top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked, press ⇧⌘G in the dialog, paste the copied path and confirm.
  4. Pin the Agenteeq icon and enter the one-time code from the app.

The extension isn’t in the Chrome Web Store yet, hence the manual steps. It sends only the conversation status and message counts, never any text, and only to the app on the same computer. You can turn off individual services at any time.

Watch your agents from your phone Wi-Fi / Tailscale
  1. Keep your Mac awake and Agenteeq running. You can close the window.
  2. In Settings → Open on phone, turn on access for your home network or for Tailscale.
  3. Click Create code for phone and point your camera at the QR code. Your phone opens already paired.

If you’d rather not scan, there’s also a six-digit number under the code. The phone sees the overview but can’t change anything, and this website can’t replace the connection to a Mac that is off.

Before you start.

How much does it cost?

The beta is free with every feature unlocked. When we leave the beta, some features will stay free; we’ll say in advance what will be paid, so nobody gets a surprise bill.

Does it show exact spend across all subscriptions?

Tokens, limits and money are three different things. You enter subscriptions and purchased credits by hand; supported Admin APIs can add verified costs. Agenteeq doesn’t turn tokens into an imagined price.

Do I have to connect every service?

No. Start with the tool you use most. The extension, hooks and other sources are optional, and you can see each connector’s availability in Settings.

What about Windows?

The Windows app is built and launched automatically with every change: the interface loads in its window and the server stops when the app closes. The package is attached to every release on GitHub as a beta. Notifications, the tray icon and other native controls still need a person to try them on a real PC, so the button on this page only offers the Mac version for now.
